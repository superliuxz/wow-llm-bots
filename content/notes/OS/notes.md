# The Obsidian Sanctum (OS)

## Sartharion

- **Shadron**: priority add; switch and burn promptly.

```
Shadron appears -> target nearest Shadron
```

## The Obsidian Sanctum

- **Shadow Breath**: boss cast; follow standing order (`out of it`).
- **Cleave**: boss cast; follow standing order (`out of it`).
- **Flame Breath**: boss cast; follow standing order (`out of it`).
- **Twilight Egg**: priority add; switch and burn promptly.

```
Shadow Breath cast -> out of it
Cleave cast -> out of it
Flame Breath cast -> out of it
Twilight Egg appears -> target nearest Twilight Egg
```
