# Icecrown Citadel (ICC)

## Lord Marrowgar

- **Saber Lash**: split between the two closest targets; both tanks stand in front of him.
- **Coldflame**: moving line of blue fire; move out immediately.
- **Bone Spike Graveyard**: impales players on **Bone Spike** targets; switch and destroy Bone Spikes immediately.
- **Bone Storm**: boss spins and deals heavy melee AoE while moving; move away from Marrowgar during Bone Storm.

```
in Coldflame -> out of it
Bone Spike appears -> target nearest Bone Spike
Bone Storm cast -> away Lord Marrowgar 15
```


## Lady Deathwhisper

- **Vengeful Shade**: react immediately (`away Vengeful Shade 15`).

```
Vengeful Shade appears -> away Vengeful Shade 15
```

## Icecrown Gunship Battle (Muradin Bronzebeard, High Overlord Saurfang, Skybreaker Sorcerer, Kor'kron Battle-Mage)

- **Skybreaker Cannon** / **Orgrim's Hammer Cannon**: cannon crew fires **Cannon Blast** to build heat and **Incinerating Blast** to vent heat before overheating and sink the enemy gunship.
- **Below Zero**: cast by enemy **Skybreaker Sorcerer** (Horde fight) or **Kor'kron Battle-Mage** (Alliance fight) to freeze your cannons; boarding party uses **Goblin Rocket Pack** to jump across, kill the mage, and return before **Battle Fury** stacks too high on the enemy commander.
- **Rocket Artillery**: red impact circles on deck; move out immediately. Defend your deck against boarding **Skybreaker Sergeant** / **Kor'kron Sergeant** and marine adds.

```
Skybreaker Sorcerer appears -> target nearest Skybreaker Sorcerer
Kor'kron Battle-Mage appears -> target nearest Kor'kron Battle-Mage
in Rocket Artillery -> out of it
```

## Deathbringer Saurfang

- **Rune of Blood**: debuff on the current tank; the other tank taunts Saurfang at once (tank swap).
- **Blood Beast**: priority add; switch and burn promptly.

```
Rune of Blood on tank & me offtank -> wake
Rune of Blood on offtank & me tank & not me offtank -> wake
Blood Beast appears -> target nearest Blood Beast
```

## Festergut

- **Gastric Bloat**: stacking debuff on the current tank (explodes fatally at 10 stacks); the other tank taunts at 8-9 stacks (`stacks>7`) for a tank swap.
- **Gas Spore** & **Inoculated**: players afflicted with **Gas Spore** stack with the raid before detonation so everyone gains **Inoculated** stacks (needed to survive **Pungent Blight** after 3 stacks of **Inhale Blight**).
- **Vile Gas**: ranged and healers stay spread at least 8 yd apart so **Vile Gas** does not splash and disorient nearby allies.
- **Malleable Goo**: green slime impact (Heroic); move out immediately.

```
Gastric Bloat stacks>7 on tank & me offtank -> wake
Gastric Bloat stacks>7 on offtank & me tank & not me offtank -> wake
Gas Spore on me -> to group
in Malleable Goo -> out of it
```

## Rotface

- **Unstable Ooze Explosion**: cast by Big Ooze; move out immediately and return once safe (`out of it, then back`).

```
Unstable Ooze Explosion cast -> out of it, then back
```

## Professor Putricide

- **Mutated Plague**: stacks on the current tank; the other tank taunts at 3-4 stacks (tank swap).
- **Slime Puddle**: ground hazard; move out immediately.
- **Choking Gas Explosion**: ground hazard; move out immediately.
- **Volatile Ooze**: priority add; switch and burn promptly.
- **Gas Cloud**: stay clear (`away Gas Cloud 14`).

```
Mutated Plague stacks>2 on tank & me offtank -> wake
Mutated Plague stacks>2 on offtank & me tank & not me offtank -> wake
in Slime Puddle -> out of it
in Choking Gas Explosion -> out of it
Volatile Ooze appears -> target nearest Volatile Ooze
Gas Cloud within 10 -> away Gas Cloud 14
```

## Blood Prince Council (Prince Valanar, Prince Keleseth, Prince Taldaram)

- **Dark Nucleus**: priority add; switch and burn promptly.
- **Empowered Shock Vortex**: boss cast; follow standing order (`out of it`).
- **Kinetic Bomb**: priority add; switch and burn promptly.
- **Ball of Flames Visual**: unempowered chasing flame orb summoned by Prince Taldaram; stay clear (`away Ball of Flames Visual 16`). Empowered orbs pulse **Empowered Flare** (`10 yd`); soak briefly if assigned to shrink their explosion before impact.

```
Dark Nucleus appears -> target nearest Dark Nucleus
Empowered Shock Vortex cast -> out of it
Kinetic Bomb appears -> target nearest Kinetic Bomb
Ball of Flames Visual within 12 -> away Ball of Flames Visual 16
```

## Blood-Queen Lana'thel

- **Blood Mirror**: links the main tank to the closest ally; off-tank stands directly beside the main tank to soak mirrored damage.
- **Essence of the Blood Queen** / **Frenzied Bloodthirst**: bitten DPS gain +100% damage; when **Frenzied Bloodthirst** triggers (10s), immediately use **Vampiric Bite** on an unbitten ally or be mind-controlled.
- **Pact of the Darkfallen**: links 2-3 players with shadow beams; linked players run together (`to ally with Pact of the Darkfallen`) to break the link.
- **Swarming Shadows**: spawns purple fire under the targeted player; immediately run away from the raid along the room edge (`away group 12`) and move out of fire puddles.
- **Bloodbolt Whirl**: during air phase after **Incite Terror** fear, spread 6+ yd apart so splash bolts do not chain.

```
Pact of the Darkfallen on me -> to ally with Pact of the Darkfallen
Swarming Shadows on me -> away group 12
in Swarming Shadows -> out of it
Frenzied Bloodthirst on me -> wake
```

## Valithria Dreamwalker

- **Blistering Zombie**: fixates on a player and explodes in melee; that player kites it while ranged kill it. Tanks never pick it up.
- **Gluttonous Abomination**, **Risen Archmage**: tanks pick these up.

```
Blistering Zombie targets me -> kite it
```

## Sindragosa

- **Ice Tomb**: damage tombs down to ~50% HP and hold them for line-of-sight cover during Frost Bomb before finishing them (decide per tomb rather than auto-burning all tombs).
- **Blistering Cold**: boss cast; follow standing order (`out of it`).

```
Blistering Cold cast -> out of it
```

## The Lich King

- **Soul Reaper**: on the current tank; the other tank taunts the Lich King at once (tank swap).
- **Remorseless Winter** & **Quake**: during transitions (70% and 40%), the Lich King channels **Remorseless Winter** from the center while the raid fights on the outer ring and kills **Raging Spirit** / **Ice Sphere** adds. When he casts **Quake**, the outer platform edge shatters — immediately run INWARD to the center (`to boss`) so you do not fall off.
- **Defile**: targets a random player and drops an expanding black pool that grows every time anyone stands in it; if targeted, run away from the raid immediately (`away group 15`), and everyone must step out of **Defile** at once (`in Defile -> out of it`).
- **Val'kyr Shadowguard**: swoops down in Phase 2, grabs a player, and carries them toward the platform edge; stack near the center so they have further to fly, switch all DPS immediately (`target nearest Val'kyr Shadowguard`), and slow or stun them before they drop the player.

```
Soul Reaper on tank & me offtank -> wake
Soul Reaper on offtank & me tank & not me offtank -> wake
Quake cast -> to boss
Defile cast on me -> away group 15
in Defile -> out of it
Val'kyr Shadowguard appears -> target nearest Val'kyr Shadowguard
```
