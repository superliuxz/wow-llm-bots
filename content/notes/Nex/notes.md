# The Nexus (Nex)

## Horde Commander (Alliance Commander, Commander Stoutbeard, Commander Kolurg)

- **Whirlwind**: boss cast; follow standing order (`out of it`).

```
Whirlwind cast -> out of it
```

## Grand Magus Telestra

- **Fire Magus**: splits into Fire, Frost, and Arcane clones at 50% (and 10% Heroic); burn **Fire Magus** first and move away from **Gravity Well**.
- **Blizzard**: frost ground hazard from Frost Magus; move out immediately.

```
Fire Magus appears -> target nearest Fire Magus
in Blizzard -> out of it
```

## Anomalus

- Periodically becomes immune and summons a **Chaotic Rift**; switch immediately and destroy **Chaotic Rift**.

```
Chaotic Rift appears -> target nearest Chaotic Rift
```

## Ormorok the Tree-Shaper

- **Crystal Spikes**: erupting ground spikes; move out immediately.
- **Spell Reflection**: reflects spells cast at Ormorok; casters stop casting while active.
- **Crystalline Tangler**: cleave down rooting adds promptly.

```
in Crystal Spikes -> out of it
Spell Reflection on boss -> stop casting
```

## Keristrasza

- **Crystalfire Breath**: frontal cone; tank faces her away from the party and healers dispel **Crystal Chains**.
- **Intense Cold**: stacking frost debuff; jump or move periodically to reset stacks.

```
Intense Cold stacks>2 on me -> jump
```
