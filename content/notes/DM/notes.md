# The Deadmines (DM)

## Rhahk'Zor

- **Rhahk'Zor's Slam**: tank faces him away and keeps threat after the stun.

## Sneed's Shredder

- **Eject Sneed**: kill Sneed's Shredder first, then pick up and kill Sneed when he ejects.

## Gilnid

- **Molten Metal**: tank holds Gilnid steady and healers watch tank damage.

## Mr. Smite

- **Smite Stomp**: stuns the party at 66% and 33% HP while he swaps weapons; keep tank topped off before transitions.

## Captain Greenskin

- **Cleave**: face Greenskin away from the party and clear his guards first.

## Edwin VanCleef

- **Defias Blackguard**: switch to and burn the Defias Blackguard adds when they spawn, then finish VanCleef.

```
Defias Blackguard appears -> target nearest Defias Blackguard
```
