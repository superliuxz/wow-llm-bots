# Wailing Caverns (WC)

## Lady Anacondra

- **Healing Touch**: interrupt Healing Touch so the fight does not drag on.
- **Druid's Slumber**: dispel sleep from allies when possible.

## Lord Cobrahn

- **Healing Touch**: interrupt Healing Touch and burn Cobrahn down in serpent form.

## Lord Pythas

- **Healing Touch**: interrupt Healing Touch and focus down adds first.

## Lord Serpentis

- **Healing Touch**: interrupt Healing Touch and watch for Lightning Bolt bursts on the tank.

## Verdan the Everliving

- **Grasping Vines**: heavy melee hits; keep the tank topped off.

## Mutanus the Devourer

- **Naralex's Nightmare**: dispel sleep from the tank or healer immediately.
