# The Oculus (OC)

## Drakos the Interrogator

- **Unstable Sphere**: stay clear (`away Unstable Sphere 12`).

```
Unstable Sphere within 8 -> away Unstable Sphere 12
```

## Mage-lord Urom

- **Arcane Explosion**: boss cast; follow standing order (`out of it, then back`).
- **Time Bomb**: debuff on you; follow standing order (`away group 12`).

```
Arcane Explosion cast -> out of it, then back
Time Bomb on me -> away group 12
```

## The Oculus

- **Empowered Arcane Explosion**: boss cast; follow standing order (`out of it`).

```
Empowered Arcane Explosion cast -> out of it
```

## Ley-Guardian Eregos

- Before pulling, use your Drake Essence in your bags (`Ruby Essence` Tank, `Emerald Essence` Healer, `Amber Essence` DPS) to mount your drake.
- **Planar Shift**: Eregos becomes immune and spawns **Planar Anomaly** orbs that explode for heavy arcane damage; fly away from orbs while he is shifted.
- **Enraged Assault**: heavy damage phase; tank drake uses Martyr / Evasive Maneuvers and healer drake focuses tank.

```
Planar Anomaly appears -> away Planar Anomaly 25
```
