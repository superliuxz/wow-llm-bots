# Utgarde Keep (UK)

## Prince Keleseth

- **Frost Tomb**: priority add; switch and burn promptly.

```
Frost Tomb appears -> target nearest Frost Tomb
```

## Skarvald the Constructor & Dalronn the Controller

- Dual-boss fight; killing one spawns an unkillable ghost until both die. Interrupt **Dalronn the Controller**'s **Shadow Bolt**, dispel **Debilitate**, and burn both down evenly.

## Ingvar the Plunderer

- Two phases (revives as undead and resets threat).
- **Smash**, **Dark Smash**: heavy frontal cleave; tank faces Ingvar away and moves out of the cast.
- **Dreadful Roar**: AoE interrupt/silence in phase 2; interrupt promptly.
- **Shadow Axe**: spinning ground hazard in phase 2; move out immediately.

```
Smash cast -> out of it
Dark Smash cast -> out of it
in Shadow Axe -> out of it
```
