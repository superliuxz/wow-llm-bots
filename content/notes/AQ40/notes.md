# Ahn'Qiraj Temple (AQ40)

## The Prophet Skeram

- Splits into copies at 75%, 50%, and 25% HP; interrupt **Arcane Explosion** and **Earth Shock** and crowd-control allies hit by **True Fulfillment**.

## Silithid Royalty (Lord Kri, Princess Yauj, Vem)

- Tank all three separately; interrupt **Princess Yauj**'s **Great Heal** and move out of **Poison Cloud** when **Lord Kri** dies.

```
in Poison Cloud -> out of it
```

## Battleguard Sartura

- Kill the three **Sartura's Royal Guard** adds first; stun during **Whirlwind** and stay clear of spinning bosses.

```
Whirlwind cast -> out of it
```

## Fankriss the Unyielding

- **Mortal Wound**: stacking healing-reduction debuff on the current tank; the other tank taunts at 3-4 stacks (tank swap).
- **Spawn of Fankriss**: priority add; switch and burn promptly.

```
Mortal Wound stacks>2 on tank & me offtank -> use Taunt on boss
Mortal Wound stacks>2 on offtank & me tank & not me offtank -> use Taunt on boss
Spawn of Fankriss appears -> target nearest Spawn of Fankriss
```

## Viscidus

- Freeze with frost damage and shatter with fast melee hits; burn **Glob of Viscidus** adds before they re-merge.

```
Glob of Viscidus appears -> target nearest Glob of Viscidus
```

## Princess Huhuran

- Dispel **Wyvern Sting** from tanks and healers, tranquilize **Frenzy**, and burn fast below 30% HP.

## The Twin Emperors (Emperor Vek'lor, Emperor Vek'nilash)

- Shared health pool; keep **Emperor Vek'lor** (physical-immune, caster DPS) and **Emperor Vek'nilash** (spell-immune, physical DPS) separated so they do not heal each other.
- **Twin Teleport**: swaps positions and resets threat; tanks re-establish threat immediately.
- **Blizzard**: ground hazard; move out immediately.

```
in Blizzard -> out of it
```

## Ouro

- **Sand Blast**: frontal cone and threat wipe; tank faces Ouro away from the raid and off-tank stays second on threat.
- **Dirt Mound**: during submerge, stay clear of erupting mounds (`away Dirt Mound 14`).

```
Dirt Mound within 10 -> away Dirt Mound 14
```

## C'Thun

- **Eye of C'Thun**: in phase 1, spread 10+ yd so **Eye Beam** does not chain; avoid **Dark Glare** and kill **Eye Tentacle** and **Claw Tentacle** adds.
- **Giant Eye Tentacle**, **Flesh Tentacle**: in phase 2, kill **Giant Eye Tentacle** and **Giant Claw Tentacle** outside, and **Flesh Tentacle** inside the stomach to weaken C'Thun.

```
Giant Eye Tentacle appears -> target nearest Giant Eye Tentacle
Flesh Tentacle appears -> target nearest Flesh Tentacle
```
