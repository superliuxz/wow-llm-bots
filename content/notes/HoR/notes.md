# Halls of Reflection (HoR)

## Falric

- **Quivering Strike**: keep the tank topped off and dispel/heal through Hopelessness stacks.
- **Defiling Horror**: party-wide fear and shadow damage; top everyone off before 66% and 33% HP.

## Marwyn

- **Well of Corruption**: move out of Well of Corruption immediately.

```
in Well of Corruption -> out of it
```

- **Corrupted Flesh**: reduces max HP; healers keep the tank stable through burst damage.

## The Lich King

- **Escape Event**: stay ahead of The Lich King, focus down Raging Ghoul and Lumbering Abomination waves at each ice wall, and avoid Remorseless Winter.

```
in Remorseless Winter -> out of it
```
