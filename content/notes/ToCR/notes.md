# Trial of the Crusader (ToCR)

## Northrend Beasts (Gormok the Impaler, Acidmaw, Dreadscale, Icehowl)

- **Impale**: stacking bleed on the current tank from Gormok the Impaler; the other tank taunts at 3 stacks (tank swap).
- **Fire Bomb**: ground fire patch; move out immediately.
- **Snobold Vassal**: priority add that interrupts casters; switch and burn promptly.
- **Acidmaw**, **Dreadscale**: tank each worm separately; players with **Burning Bile** move to **Paralytic Toxin** players to clear the stun.
- **Icehowl**: tank faces him away for **Arctic Breath**; after **Massive Crash** knocks the raid back, move out of his charge path and re-taunt immediately after.

```
Impale stacks>2 on tank & me offtank -> wake
Impale stacks>2 on offtank & me tank & not me offtank -> wake
in Fire Bomb -> out of it
Snobold Vassal appears -> target nearest Snobold Vassal
```

## Lord Jaraxxus

- **Legion Flame**: drops fire under the targeted player; run away from the group and stay out of fire.
- **Incinerate Flesh**: heal-absorb debuff on a raid member; healers burst-heal the target immediately before it explodes.
- **Nether Power**: dispellable magic-damage buff on Jaraxxus; dispel or spellsteal promptly and interrupt **Fel Fireball**.
- **Nether Portal**, **Mistress of Pain**, **Infernal Volcano**: off-tank picks up spawned adds; DPS switches to portals, volcanoes, and Mistress of Pain immediately.

```
Legion Flame on me -> away group 15
in Legion Flame -> out of it
Nether Portal appears -> target nearest Nether Portal
Mistress of Pain appears -> target nearest Mistress of Pain
Infernal Volcano appears -> target nearest Infernal Volcano
```

## Faction Champions

- PvP-style encounter with no standard threat table; focus enemy healers first, interrupt casts, dispel crowd control, and peel melee off friendly healers.

## Twin Val'kyr (Fjola Lightbane, Eydis Darkbane)

- Shared health pool; tanks hold **Fjola Lightbane** and **Eydis Darkbane** separately.
- Match **Light Essence** or **Dark Essence** to survive **Light Vortex** / **Dark Vortex** and deal bonus damage to the opposite twin.
- **Twins' Pact**: switch to the casting twin, break the shield, and interrupt the heal.

## Anub'arak

- **Nerubian Burrower**: off-tank picks up burrowers and tanks them on **Permafrost** ice patches so they cannot submerge; DPS burns them down and interrupts **Shadow Strike**.
- **Pursuing Spikes**: during submerge phase, the chased player kites spikes across **Permafrost** patches to break pursuit.
- **Leeching Swarm**: below 30% HP, drains raid HP to heal Anub'arak; focus heavy healing on tanks and **Penetrating Cold** targets while keeping non-tanks low.

```
Nerubian Burrower appears -> target nearest Nerubian Burrower
```
