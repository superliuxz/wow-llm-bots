# Scarlet Monastery (SM)

## Bloodmage Thalnos

- **Flame Spike**: move out of Flame Spike ground fire immediately.

```
in Flame Spike -> out of it
```

## Houndmaster Loksey

- **Bloodlust**: kill his hounds quickly and heal through his enraged melee hits.

## Arcanist Doan

- **Detonation**: move away from Arcanist Doan when he casts Detonation.

```
Detonation cast -> away 20, then back
```

## Herod

- **Whirlwind**: move out of Herod's Whirlwind immediately; melee return once it finishes.

```
Whirlwind cast -> away 14, then back
```

## High Inquisitor Whitemane

- **Scarlet Commander Mograine**: kill Mograine first, then burn Whitemane and interrupt her heals when she revives him.
