# Naxxramas (Naxx)

## Patchwerk

- Pure tank-and-spank throughput check.
- **Hateful Strike**: hits the highest-HP melee target after the main tank; off-tank stays in melee range and healers keep both tanks topped.

## Anub'Rekhan

- **Locust Swarm**: boss moves slowly and deals heavy nature AoE around himself; main tank kites Anub'Rekhan around the room perimeter while DPS kills **Crypt Guard** adds.

## Maexxna

- **Web Wrap**: cocoons players against the wall; ranged DPS breaks **Web Wrap** immediately.
- **Maexxna Spiderling**: spawns small spiders; AoE them down before Web Spray.

```
Web Wrap appears -> target nearest Web Wrap
```

## Thaddius

- **Polarity Shift**: assigns Positive or Negative Charge; stack with same-charge players and stay away from opposite-charge players.

## Sapphiron

- **Blizzard**: moving frost AoE; move out immediately.
- **Frost Breath**: during air phase, hide behind an **Ice Block** before Frost Breath lands.

```
in Blizzard -> out of it
```

## Heigan the Unclean

- **Eruption**: floor sections erupt in a repeating wave (Safety Dance); move out of erupting zones immediately and follow the tank across the safe zone in both phases.
- **Decrepit Fever**: dispel disease promptly; tank holds Heigan away from the platform so ranged avoid **Spell Disruption**.

```
in Eruption -> out of it
```

## Gluth

- **Mortal Wound**: stacking healing-reduction debuff on the current tank; the other tank taunts at 3 stacks (tank swap).
- **Zombie Chow**: off-tank/kiters keep them away from Gluth; after **Decimate** drops them to 5% HP, burn them down before they reach Gluth.

```
Mortal Wound stacks>2 on tank & me offtank -> wake
Mortal Wound stacks>2 on offtank & me tank & not me offtank -> wake
```

## The Four Horsemen (Thane Korth'azz, Lady Blaumeux, Sir Zeliek, Baron Rivendare)

- **Corner Positioning**: each horseman holds a room corner — **Thane Korth'azz** and **Baron Rivendare** in the front corners (melee), **Lady Blaumeux** and **Sir Zeliek** in the back corners (casters). Every horseman must always have a target in range or they wipe the raid.
- **Mark of Korth'azz**, **Mark of Rivendare**, **Mark of Blaumeux**, **Mark of Zeliek**: proximity aura stacking every ~12s within ~45 yd; taunting in place does NOT clear marks. Rotate corners and move out of your previous horseman's aura range at 3 stacks (`stacks>2`) so marks drop while another tank/soaker takes that corner.
- **Meteor**: cast by Thane Korth'azz; splits fire damage among nearby players, so melee stack with Korth'azz's tank to soak it.
- **Void Zone**: dark ground hazard cast by Lady Blaumeux; move out immediately.
- **Holy Wrath**: cast by Sir Zeliek; chains to nearby players with escalating damage, so spread out near Zeliek.

```
Mark of Korth'azz stacks>2 on me -> wake
Mark of Rivendare stacks>2 on me -> wake
Mark of Blaumeux stacks>2 on me -> wake
Mark of Zeliek stacks>2 on me -> wake
in Void Zone -> out of it
```

## Kel'Thuzad

- Phase 1: kill **Soldier of the Frozen Wastes**, **Unstoppable Abomination**, and **Soul Weaver** adds before they overwhelm the center.
- **Shadow Fissure**: fatal dark ground crack; move out immediately.
- **Frostbolt**: interrupt single-target **Frostbolt** on the tank; spread 10+ yd so **Frost Blast** does not chain-freeze allies.
- **Guardian of Icecrown**: at 45% HP, off-tank picks up guardians and tanks them away from the raid while DPS finishes Kel'Thuzad.

```
in Shadow Fissure -> out of it
```
