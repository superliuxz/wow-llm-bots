# The Ruby Sanctum (RS)

## The Ruby Sanctum

- **Living Inferno**: priority add; switch and burn promptly.
- **Flame Breath**: boss cast; follow standing order (`out of it`).
- **Cleave**: boss cast; follow standing order (`out of it`).
- **Dark Breath**: boss cast; follow standing order (`out of it`).

```
Living Inferno appears -> target nearest Living Inferno
Flame Breath cast -> out of it
Cleave cast -> out of it
Dark Breath cast -> out of it
```
