# Blackrock Depths (BRD)

## High Interrogator Gerstahn

- **Shadow Word: Pain**: dispel magic debuffs and interrupt Mana Burn.

## Lord Roccor

- **Earthquake**: heal through AoE damage and keep him away from patrols.

## Bael'Gar

- **Spawn of Bael'Gar**: kill Spawn of Bael'Gar adds quickly and keep melee out of magma pools.

## General Angerforge

- **Enrage**: clear surrounding packs before pulling and pick up his summoned guards promptly.

## Golem Lord Argelmach

- **Chain Lightning**: spread out slightly and clear the room golems before pulling.

## Ambassador Flamelash

- **Burning Spirit**: switch to and kill Burning Spirit adds before they reach Ambassador Flamelash.

```
Burning Spirit appears -> target nearest Burning Spirit
```

## Emperor Dagran Thaurissan

- **Avatar of Flame**: tank holds steady threat and interrupts Princess Moira Bronzebeard's heals.
