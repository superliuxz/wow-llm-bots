# Scholomance (Scholo)

## Jandice Barov

- **Illusion of Jandice Barov**: AoE or pick off illusions until the real Jandice Barov is found.

## Rattlegore

- **War Stomp**: heavy melee hits and knockback; tank with your back to a wall.

## Ras Frostwhisper

- **Frostbolt Volley**: interrupt Frostbolt Volley when possible and keep the party topped off.

## Instructor Malicia

- **Heal**: interrupt her heals and focus her down.

## Doctor Theolen Krastinov

- **Frenzy**: tank uses defensive cooldowns and healers focus the tank during Frenzy.

## Lorekeeper Polkelt

- **Volatile Infection**: dispel disease and spread out slightly.

## Darkmaster Gandling

- **Shadow Portal**: if teleported into a side room, clear the skeletons to unlock the door and rejoin the group.
