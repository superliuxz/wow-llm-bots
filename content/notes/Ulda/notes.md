# Uldaman (Ulda)

## Revelosh

- **Lightning Bolt** and **Chain Lightning**: 3s casts; interrupt when possible and spread slightly for Chain Lightning.

## Ironaya

- **Arcing Smash**: instant frontal cleave; tank faces Ironaya away from the party.
- **Knock Away** and **War Stomp**: melee knockback with threat drop and AoE stun; ranged and healers stay out of melee range.

## Obsidian Sentinel

- **Obsidian Shard**: summons **Obsidian Shard** adds during combat; switch and burn shards promptly.
- **Reflection** and **Splintered Obsidian**: self-buffs during combat; keep steady pressure on the boss between shards.

```
Obsidian Shard appears -> target nearest Obsidian Shard
```

## Ancient Stone Keeper

- **Sand Storm**: summons wandering **Sand Storm** hazards that pulse AoE damage; stay clear of them.

```
Sand Storm within 8 -> away Sand Storm 12
```

## Galgann Firehammer

- **Fire Nova**: 2s cast dealing heavy AoE fire damage within 10 yd; step out of melee range during the cast.
- **Amplify Flames** and **Flame Lash**: short fire casts; interrupt when possible.

```
Fire Nova cast -> away boss 12
```

## Grimlok

- **Lightning Bolt**, **Chain Bolt**, and **Shrink**: 2.5–3s casts; interrupt casts and dispel Shrink.
- **Bloodlust**: casts at 40% HP; keep the tank topped through the haste burst.

## Archaedas

- **Awaken Earthen Dwarf**: periodically awakens stone adds (**Earthen Custodian** and **Earthen Hallshaper**); interrupt **Earthen Hallshaper** heals and cleave adds down.
- **Awaken Earthen Guardians** (70% HP) and **Awaken Vault Warder** (40% HP): awakens stronger stone guardians (**Earthen Guardian** with Whirlwind, **Vault Warder** with Trample); tank picks up awakened adds while DPS burns them or finishes Archaedas.
- **Ground Tremor**: instant AoE stun; healers keep the tank topped through the stun.
