# Stratholme (Strat)

## The Unforgiven

- **Vengeful Phantom**: spawns four **Vengeful Phantom** adds on pull; cleave and burn phantoms promptly.
- **Frost Nova**: instant melee root; ranged stay at range and dispel roots on melee.

```
Vengeful Phantom appears -> target nearest Vengeful Phantom
```

## Hearthsinger Forresten

- **Enchanting Lullaby**: 1s cast sleep on a party member; interrupt or dispel promptly.

## Timmy the Cruel

- **Enrage**: enrages at 50% HP for heavy melee damage; healers keep the tank topped.

## Cannon Master Willey

- **Crimson Rifleman**: periodically summons **Crimson Rifleman** adds; switch and kill riflemen promptly.
- **Knock Away** and **Pummel**: melee knockback and interrupt; ranged and healers stay out of melee range.

```
Crimson Rifleman appears -> target nearest Crimson Rifleman
```

## Archivist Galford

- **Pyroblast**: 6s cast heavy fire nuke; interrupt **Pyroblast** immediately.
- **Burning Winds**: 1s cast stun and fire DoT; interrupt or dispel when possible.
- **Fire Nova**: instant melee fire AoE; ranged and healers stay out of melee range.

## Grand Crusader Dathrohan / Balnazzar

- **Crusader's Hammer**: instant melee AoE stun; ranged and healers stay out of melee range.
- **Balnazzar Transform**: transforms into **Balnazzar** at 40% HP.
- **Mind Blast** and **Sleep**: 1–1.5s casts in Balnazzar form; interrupt casts and dispel **Sleep**.
- **Mind Control** and **Psychic Scream**: instant charm and AoE fear; dispel **Mind Control** promptly and stay spread.

## Magistrate Barthilas

- **Crowd Pummel** and **Mighty Blow**: instant melee knockbacks; tank positions with back to a wall and ranged stay out of melee range.
- **Furious Anger**: stacking damage buff; burn quickly and keep the tank topped.

## Baroness Anastari

- **Possess**: mind-controls a party member and turns invisible; damage or CC the possessed ally until **Possess** breaks below 50% HP without killing them.
- **Banshee Curse**: hit-chance reduction curse; dispel curses when available.
- **Silence** and **Banshee Wail**: 1.5s shadow casts; interrupt when possible and stay spread.

## Nerub'enkan

- **Undead Scarab**: casts **Raise Undead Scarab** (1s cast) summoning **Undead Scarab** adds; switch and burn scarabs promptly.
- **Encasing Webs**: 2s cast root; interrupt or dispel promptly.

```
Undead Scarab appears -> target nearest Undead Scarab
```

## Maleki the Pallid

- **Frostbolt** and **Ice Tomb**: 1.5–2s frost casts; interrupt casts and dispel **Ice Tomb**.
- **Drain Life** and **Banshee Curse**: interrupt **Drain Life** and dispel curses.

## Ramstein the Gorger

- **Knockout** and **Trample**: instant melee stun with threat drop and AoE stomp; ranged and healers stay out of melee range.

## Baron Rivendare

- **Mindless Skeleton**: casts **Raise Dead** summoning **Mindless Skeleton** adds; switch and cleave skeletons down quickly before they overwhelm the group or heal the boss.
- **Cleave** and **Mortal Strike**: heavy frontal cleave and healing-reduction debuff on the tank; tank faces Baron Rivendare away from the party and healers keep the tank topped through **Unholy Aura**.
- **Shadow Bolt**: 2s cast; interrupt when possible.

```
Mindless Skeleton appears -> target nearest Mindless Skeleton
```
