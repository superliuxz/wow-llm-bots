# Trial of the Champion (TOC)

## Argent Confessor Paletress

- **Reflective Shield**: at 50% HP she shields herself and summons a **Memory of the Past**; stop hitting Paletress, switch to **Memory of the Past**, and burn it down so her shield drops.
- **Renew**: dispel immediately; interrupt **Holy Smite**.

```
Memory of the Past appears -> target nearest Memory of the Past
```

## Eadric the Pure

- **Radiance**: blinds and damages anyone facing him; turn away before the cast finishes.

```
Radiance cast -> face away boss, then stop attacking
```

## The Black Knight

- Three phases (undead, skeleton, ghost): burn through each phase.
- **Desecration**: dark ground pool in phase 2; move out immediately.
- **Risen Ghoul**: kill ghouls quickly and step away before **Ghoul Explode**.
- **Mark of the Death Knight**: amplifies damage taken in phase 3; healers focus the tank.

```
in Desecration -> out of it
```
