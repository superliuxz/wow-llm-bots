# Gundrak (GD)

## Slad'ran

- **Poison Nova**: boss cast; follow standing order (`out of it`).
- **Snake Wrap**: priority add; switch and burn promptly.

```
Poison Nova cast -> out of it
Snake Wrap appears -> target nearest Snake Wrap
```

## Drakkari Colossus

- Splits at 50% and 20% HP into **Drakkari Elemental** while the Colossus is immune; switch to **Drakkari Elemental** immediately.
- **Mojo Puddle**: ground hazard; move out immediately.

```
Drakkari Elemental appears -> target nearest Drakkari Elemental
in Mojo Puddle -> out of it
```

## Moorabi

- **Transformation**: interrupt immediately so he does not transform into a mammoth; healers top the party after **Ground Tremor**.

## Eck the Ferocious

- **Eck Spring**: leaps to a random player and drops threat; tank re-taunts promptly and faces him away for **Eck Residue**.

## Gal'darah

- Swaps between troll and rhino form; melee move out of **Whirling Slash**, and healers watch **Puncture** bleed on the tank and **Impaling Charge** targets.

```
Whirling Slash cast -> out of it
```
