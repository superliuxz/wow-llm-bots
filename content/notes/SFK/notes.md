# Shadowfang Keep (SFK)

## Rethilgore

- **Soul Drain**: interrupt Soul Drain if possible and burn him down.

## Razorclaw the Butcher

- **Butcher Drain**: keep tank HP high through heavy melee hits.

## Baron Silverlaine

- **Veil of Shadow**: reduces healing taken by 75%; decurse the tank promptly.

## Commander Springvale

- **Holy Light**: interrupt Holy Light and clear his guards first.

## Fenrus the Devourer

- **Toxic Saliva**: dispel poison on melee if available.

## Wolf Master Nandos

- **Bleak Worg**: burn summoned worgs quickly so the healer does not pull aggro.

## Archmage Arugal

- **Arugal's Curse**: transforms a party member; control or wait out the mind-controlled ally and focus Arugal when he teleports.
