# Pit of Saron (PoS)

## Ick

- **Pursuit**: fixate debuff on a target; kite Ick away while pursued (`kite boss`).
- **Poison Nova**: boss cast; follow standing order (`out of it`).
- **Toxic Waste**: green poison pools on the ground; move out immediately.

```
Pursuit on me -> kite boss
Poison Nova cast -> out of it
in Toxic Waste -> out of it
```
