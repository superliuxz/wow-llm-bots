## High-Value Diagnostics Exposed by the 10-Boss Suite

1. **Movement / Dodge radius gap (`Murmur` & `Ick`):**
   - On **Murmur**, `Sonic Boom` hits out to **34 yd**; none of the 5 bots ran past `24.6 yd` (`0.0–24.6 yd`), causing a full 5-man wipe at `49.8 s`.
   - On **Ick**, only `Loade` (Hunter, `30.9 yd`) created distance during casts; the rest stayed `0–10.4 yd` and ate `Poison Nova` / `Pursuit`.
2. **Bag item use for pre-fight vehicle mounting (`Ley-Guardian Eregos`):**
   - All 5 bots had their Drake Essence (`Ruby`/`Emerald`/`Amber Essence`) in their bags, and `notes.md` told them to use it before pulling, yet none opened bags and used the item to mount their drakes.
3. **Raid tanking / Hateful Strike soaking (`Patchwerk` 10-man):**
   - The 10-man raid sustained **12.7k party DPS** and **7.2k party HPS** (`Loadb` Priest 3,626 HPS, `Loadg` Druid 2,429 HPS) for 51.5 s — very close to viable, but `Loada` (main tank) only took 58k dmg while `Loadf` (Paladin) took 127k and `Loadi` (DPS Warrior) took 107k, showing off-tank/melee Hateful Strike soak and tank-healer focus need work (or a 3rd healer / proper tank spec).
4. **Encounter setup notes for 2 Classic bosses:**
   - **Darkmaster Gandling** (`Scholomance`) doesn't engage until the 6 side-room mini-bosses are dead (swap to **Rattlegore** or **Kirtonos the Herald** for a direct Classic pull).
   - **Emperor Dagran Thaurissan** (`BRD`) is surrounded by the entire Imperial Seat trash pack (either clear the room or swap to **General Drakkisath** / **Princess Theradras**).
